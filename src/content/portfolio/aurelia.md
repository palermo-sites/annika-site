Aurelia began with a single fold. The neckline is one continuous length of mikado, pressed and pinned over three fittings until it sat like a frame rather than a strap.

The skirt carries deep inverted pleats from the natural waist so the gown keeps its line standing still and opens when the bride walks. A silk organza underlayer gives the mikado its lift without crinoline.
