Every motif on Seraphine was cut from the lace by hand and placed on the tulle one at a time, so the flowers climb the body instead of repeating across it.

The flare begins just above the knee. It is built on a lightly boned crepe-backed satin foundation that lets the lace sit flat against the figure.
