Crepe hides nothing. Without lace or beading, every seam, dart and hem is visible. That is exactly why it photographs so beautifully: light moves across it in long, even gradients.

Cutting on the bias lets crepe skim the hip and fall straight to the floor. It also lets it grow, so every bias seam hangs overnight before it is hemmed.
