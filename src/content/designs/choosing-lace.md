Lace is chosen with the hands first. Chantilly is fine and soft and drapes like a second skin. Alençon is corded, so its motifs stand up and hold a scalloped edge. Guipure has no net at all, which makes it bold and graphic.

For a fitted silhouette I prefer a lace that can be cut apart and re-placed by hand, so the pattern follows the seams rather than fighting them.
