A full tulle skirt can weigh more than it looks. The corset carries that weight at the waist, not the shoulders.

Spiral steel bones bend with the body. A grosgrain waist stay takes the strain off the outer fabric. A cotton coutil interlining keeps it all smooth beneath the satin. Done well, the bride forgets it is there.
